Product Care

Caring for Your Sandou

ac_unit

Refrigerate After Opening

Once opened, store your Sandou Kremas in the refrigerator. It keeps best between 35–40°F and will maintain its quality for up to 3 months after opening.

light_mode

Store Away from Light

Keep unopened bottles in a cool, dark place. Direct sunlight can affect the delicate balance of flavors — treat it like you'd treat a fine spirit.

rotate_left

Shake Gently Before Serving

Natural ingredients may settle over time. Give your bottle a gentle shake before pouring to ensure every pour delivers the full, rich flavor experience.

timer

Best Within 12 Months

Unopened bottles are best enjoyed within 12 months of purchase. Though with Sandou, we doubt a bottle stays unopened that long.
