The Founder's Selection

Coconut Cream

The Signature Expression

"Coconut Cream is the flavor I return to. It's the one that started everything — the one where I knew I had created something truly special. Every sip takes me back to where this all began."

— Alexandra Louis, Founder & Cellar Master

Flavor Profile

Lush tropical coconut, silky cream, whispers of vanilla and warm spice. Richly textured with a long, gentle finish.

Best Served

Chilled over ice. During celebrations. As a gift to someone you love.

Crafting Notes

—Crafted with authentic Haitian tradition, small-batch

—Silky coconut notes, layered with vanilla warmth and gentle spice

—Best served chilled, over ice or neat from the bottle
