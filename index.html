The Founder

Meet Alexandra Louis

Sandou Kremas was born from Alexandra Louis's vision — a kitchen in Port-au-Prince filled with the scent of toasted coconut and warm nutmeg. For Alexandra, this isn't just a beverage — it's a bottled memory of Haiti's most cherished celebrations.

"I wanted to create something that people had never tasted before. Every bottle tells a story — and that story begins in Port-au-Prince."

Alexandra Louis
