The Flavor Story

Bright. Tropical.
Pure Celebration.

Mango Passion was born in the open-air markets of Haiti, where ripe mangoes perfume every corridor with sweetness that exists nowhere else on earth. Alexandra created this expression as a tribute to that vivid memory — the colors, the energy, the absolute joy of a Haitian summer.

Fresh Haitian mango leads the way, sun-sweet and unmistakably real. Passion fruit arrives next, adding a bright tartness that lifts the experience into something luminous and alive. The silky coconut cream base rounds every note into seamless lushness, with a gentle rum warmth that extends the tropical moment long after the last sip.

"Mango Passion is pure joy in a glass. Every time I taste it, I'm transported back to the markets of Port-au-Prince — the colors, the energy, the sweetness in the air."

— Alexandra Louis, Founder
