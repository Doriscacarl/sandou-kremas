The Founder

Meet The Heart Behind Sandou

Sandou Kremas was born in a kitchen filled with the scent of toasted coconut and warm nutmeg. For our founder, Sandou, this isn't just a beverage; it's a bottled memory of her grandmother's porch in Jacmel.

"I remember the precision," she recalls. "The way the lime zest was folded into the cream, the patient stirring that lasted into the sunset. It was a ritual of love." Sandou has spent a decade refining these family secrets, elevating the traditional Haitian Kremas into a sophisticated spirit that honors its roots while embracing a global, luxury palate.
