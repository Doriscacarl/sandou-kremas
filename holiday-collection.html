The Flavor Story

Festive. Spiced.
Deeply Celebratory.

Each year, Alexandra crafts a special limited run of Sandou Kremas for the holiday season — a blend that goes beyond the everyday expressions. The Holiday Collection is her most personal gift: to the families who share it at Nwèl, to the friends who toast the new year, to the guests who carry a bottle home as something truly special.

Warm cinnamon opens the experience with immediate holiday welcome. Star anise and clove weave through with rich, aromatic complexity that deepens with every sip. The signature Sandou coconut cream base grounds everything in smooth luxury, while aged rum and a whisper of freshly grated nutmeg close with a warmth that lingers long into the evening.

"The Holiday Collection is my gift to the people who celebrate with us year after year. I put everything into this batch — it's the one I'm most proud of every year."

— Alexandra Louis, Founder

Availability

The Holiday Collection is produced in limited quantities each year and typically sells out before the season ends. Reserve early to guarantee your bottles.
