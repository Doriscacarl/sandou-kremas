The Flavor Story

The Flavor That
Started Everything

Coconut Cream is more than a flavor — it is the moment Alexandra knew she had created something extraordinary. When she first perfected this expression, she understood that Sandou was ready for the world.

Lush tropical coconut opens immediately, warm and enveloping. Silky cream carries it through. Whispers of vanilla and gentle spice trail behind — a finish that lingers like a beautiful memory refusing to leave.

"Coconut Cream is the one I return to. Every sip takes me back to the moment I knew Sandou was real."

— Alexandra Louis, Founder
