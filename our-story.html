Her Story

She Didn't Inherit a Recipe.
She Created One.

Alexandra Louis was born and raised in Port-au-Prince, Haiti — a city alive with color, music, and the deep aromas of Kremas flowing through celebrations of every kind. Kremas was everywhere. At every birthday, every holiday, every family gathering. But for Alexandra, it was more than a tradition. It was a calling.

She taught herself. Through years of experimentation, intuition, and an unrelenting commitment to quality, she developed a recipe that stood apart from anything else. No family secret was passed down to her — she built the secret herself, ingredient by ingredient, until every pour was exactly right.

"I wanted people to taste something and say — I've never had Kremas like this before. That was my goal from the very beginning."

Customers have said exactly that. Again and again.

Alexandra Louis

Founder & Master Blender, Sandou Kremas

The Journey

A Legacy In The Making

2013 — 2014

The First Batches

In Port-au-Prince, Haiti, Alexandra begins crafting Kremas in her kitchen. Late nights, careful measurements, and an unwavering vision of what this drink could become. Each batch better than the last.

First Sales

Laplaine, Haiti

Word travels fast when something tastes extraordinary. Alexandra's first bottles sell in Laplaine — and people come back asking for more. The dream of a real brand begins to take shape.

The Craft Years

Perfecting the Recipe

Alexandra spends years deepening her craft. Every ingredient is interrogated. Every balance reconsidered. The Sandou recipe is not rushed — it is earned through patience, passion, and relentless pursuit of excellence.

2024 — The Relaunch

Sandou Kremas Arrives in America

With years of craft and conviction behind her, Alexandra officially launches Sandou Kremas in the United States. The same recipe. A new chapter. A brand ready to introduce the world to authentic Haitian luxury.

Today

Shipping Across the Nation

Sandou Kremas now ships to homes across the United States. Every bottle carries the same care, the same integrity, and the same story that began in a kitchen in Port-au-Prince. The celebration continues.

The Vision

Building a Legacy

Alexandra's vision is clear: to build one of the most respected Haitian-owned Kremas brands in the world. To make Haitian craftsmanship celebrated globally. The story has only just begun.

A Message From Alexandra

"From My Heart
to Your Home"

Dear Friend,

Every bottle of Sandou Kremas carries a piece of me in it. When I started making Kremas in Port-au-Prince, I didn't have a business plan. I had a vision, a kitchen, and an unwillingness to settle for anything ordinary.

Haiti gave me everything. The aromas, the celebrations, the spirit of a people who know how to savor life even in its most difficult moments. Kremas was always the drink that brought people together — at Christmas, at Easter, at birthdays, at reunions. It was the drink that said: we are here, we are together, and this moment matters.

When I came to America, I brought that spirit with me. Every recipe I refined, every batch I perfected, every bottle I poured — it was all in service of one thing: sharing something real. Something from the heart of Haiti, offered with love to homes across this country.

When you open a bottle of Sandou, I hope you feel that. I hope you taste the care, the culture, and the decade of dedication that went into every pour. And I hope it brings you and your loved ones the same kind of joy it brought me in those early mornings in my kitchen in Port-au-Prince, imagining a day when the world would taste what I had created.

That day is here. Thank you for being part of this journey.

Alexandra Louis

Founder & Master Blender, Sandou Kremas

The Principles

What Alexandra Believes

The values that shaped every decision — from the first bottle in Port-au-Prince to every pour in America.

01

Heritage Is Everything

Haiti's culture, history, and flavor tradition are not just inspirations — they are the foundation. Every ingredient, every decision, every pour is an act of cultural pride.

02

Never Compromise Quality

Alexandra spent years perfecting her recipe because she refused to release anything that wasn't extraordinary. That commitment to excellence never wavers — not in the first batch, not in the thousandth.

03

Make People Feel Something

A great drink doesn't just taste good — it takes you somewhere. It brings back a memory, creates a new one, or makes the people around you feel celebrated. Sandou exists for those moments.

04

Haitian Excellence Belongs Everywhere

The finest Haitian craftsmanship deserves a seat at the world's most celebrated tables. Sandou Kremas is Alexandra's statement that Haitian luxury is real, refined, and ready for the world.

05

Build With Patience

Great brands, like great Kremas, cannot be rushed. Alexandra took years to perfect her craft because she understood that anything worth building is worth building right.

06

Share Generously

Kremas was never meant to be kept to yourself. It exists to be poured, shared, and celebrated with the people who matter most. That is the spirit of Sandou — and the spirit of Haiti itself.
